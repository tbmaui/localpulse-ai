## 1. Product Overview
- **Brand / Working Name:** LocalPulse AI
- **One-Line Description:** Done-for-you, high-converting AI websites with interactive AI Video Avatars, 24/7 Voice Phone Agents, and automated Lead & Content Machines for local businesses.
- **What It Does:** Delivers a modern, high-converting website for local service businesses (tire shops, auto mechanics, bakeries/donut shops, nail salons, contractors) backed by modular, plug-and-play AI super-skills:
  1. Interactive AI Video Avatar Host (speaks directly to visitors on the site).
  2. 24/7 AI Phone Agent (answers phone calls in natural voice, takes messages & schedules).
  3. 24/7 AI Web Chat & Appointment Concierge.
  4. Outbound Unlimited B2B Lead Machine (area + industry lead pulling engine).
  5. Automated AI SEO & Content Generation Machine.
  6. Automated 5-Star Review & Reputation Booster.
  7. Instant Missed-Call SMS Textback.
- **Business Model & Pricing:** 
  - **Base Plan:** $598 Month 1 ($299 setup & strategy + $299 Month 1), then $299/month recurring.
  - **Modular Add-ons:** $49 – $149/mo per AI capability.
  - **High Volume / Low Touch:** Automated onboarding, instant preview generation, frictionless sales motion.

## 3. Core Offer & The AI Enhancement Menu

### Core Package ($598 Month 1 / $299/mo Recurring)
- Custom-designed, ultra-fast, mobile-first local website.
- Secure cloud hosting, SSL, custom domain connection, and daily backups.
- Ongoing maintenance, menu/pricing updates, and seasonal promotion banners.
- Core local contact capture & click-to-call integration.

### High-Margin AI Enhancement Modules (Add-on Catalog)

1. **Interactive AI Live Video Avatar Host (+$89/mo):**
   - A photorealistic, customized video avatar on the website that greets visitors, explains current specials, and guides them to book or call.
2. **24/7 AI Voice Phone Receptionist (+$99/mo):**
   - Answers inbound shop phone calls 24/7 in natural human voice. Answers FAQs, checks inventory/hours, and books appointments directly into the calendar.
3. **Outbound Unlimited Local Lead Machine (+$149/mo - Premium):**
   - Targeted local B2B and consumer lead scraping engine. Business owners specify an area and industry to receive high-intent qualified leads continuously.
4. **Automated AI SEO & Content Generation Machine (+$89/mo):**
   - Autonomous content engine that writes and publishes localized keyword articles, service pages, and Google Business updates weekly.
5. **24/7 AI Chatbot & Booking Concierge (+$69/mo):**
   - Web chat trained on service menu, pricing, and FAQs that captures visitor contact info and books jobs.
6. **5-Star Review & Reputation Booster (+$49/mo):**
   - Automated post-service SMS review requests that direct happy customers to Google Maps and auto-replies to reviews.
7. **Missed-Call Instant AI Textback (+$59/mo):**
   - Instantly texts callers when the phone isn't answered in 5 seconds to prevent them from calling a competitor.

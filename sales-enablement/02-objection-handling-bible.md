## Detailed Objection Scripts

### 1. "We already have a website."
- **Why they say it:** They think you're selling a static brochure website built in 2018.
- **Script:**
> *"I totally understand [Owner Name], and I actually saw your current website. It has your address and phone number, which is great. But here's the difference: traditional websites are passive brochures. Our AI websites are active revenue engines—they come with a live talking video avatar that greets customers and a 24/7 AI voice phone agent that answers your calls when you're busy in the back. If someone visits your site after hours, can they book an appointment and get instant pricing right now?"*

---

### 2. "Send me an email / I'm busy."
- **Why they say it:** It's the standard brush-off to get off the phone.
- **Script:**
> *"Fair enough, I know you're slammed right now. I can definitely send an email, but frankly, 90% of emails end up in spam. How about this: I'll text a 30-second screen recording of your shop's custom AI preview to your cell. What's the best mobile number to send that to?"*

---

### 3. "We tried digital marketing before and got burned."
- **Why they say it:** They paid an agency $5,000 upfront or $1,500/mo retainer with zero tangible leads.
- **Script:**
> *"I hear that every single day. Traditional agencies charge $5,000 upfront, lock you into a 6-month contract, and take 8 weeks to build a site that doesn't bring in a single lead. We did the exact opposite: no long-term contracts, month-to-month for $299, live in 48 hours, and backed by a 30-day 'more leads or it's free' guarantee. If it doesn't bring you customers, you cancel and pay nothing."*

---

### 4. "I need to talk to my business partner / spouse."
- **Why they say it:** Valid objection or delaying tactic.
- **Script:**
> *"That makes total sense. Rather than trying to explain an AI video avatar and 24/7 voice receptionist over dinner, let me build the custom live preview for your shop first. That way, you both can pull it up on your phones and test it together. Does tomorrow afternoon or Thursday morning work to send that over?"*

---

### 5. "Can I just buy the website without the AI?"
- **Script:**
> *"Our core $299/mo plan includes the entire turnkey custom website, hosting, SSL, and mobile optimization. The AI add-ons like the Video Avatar, Phone Receptionist, and Outbound Lead Machine are completely modular. You can start with just the core site and toggle on the AI features whenever you're ready!"*

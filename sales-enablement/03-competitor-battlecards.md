# Competitor Battle Cards
**Brand:** LocalPulse AI  
**Purpose:** Arm outbound reps to instantly position against traditional web agencies, DIY platforms, and lead generation aggregators (Yelp, Thumbtack, Angi).

---

## Battle Card 1: LocalPulse AI vs. Traditional Web Agencies

### Competitor Overview
Traditional digital agencies / freelance web designers charging $3,000–$7,500 upfront plus $500–$1,500/mo ongoing retainers.

### The Attack Vector (Where they fail)
- **Extreme Latency:** Take 6 to 12 weeks to deliver a simple website.
- **Massive Cash Outlay:** Require $3k–$5k upfront before generating a single dollar.
- **Zero Automation:** They build static digital brochures. No live video avatars, no 24/7 AI phone answering, no automated SEO content machines.
- **Nickel & Dime:** Charge $125–$175/hr for basic menu or price text edits.

### Landmine Questions to Plant in Prospect's Mind
- *"When was the last time your web agency proactively generated 50 new B2B leads or answered your phone after hours?"*
- *"If you want to change a price or add a holiday promotion, do they bill you $150 per hour and take a week to update it?"*

### Comparison Grid

| Feature | LocalPulse AI | Traditional Agency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Upfront Cost** | **$598 (Setup + Month 1)** | $3,000 – $7,500+ |
| **Ongoing Cost** | **$299/mo (flat, month-to-month)** | $500 – $1,500/mo retainer |
| **Turnaround** | **Live in 48 Hours** | 6 to 12 Weeks |
| **AI Video Avatar & Phone** | **Built-in & Integrated** | Not offered or custom $5k+ quote |
| **Content & SEO Machine** | **Continuous Automated Publishing** | Manual, expensive add-on |
| **Contract** | **Month-to-month, cancel anytime** | 6 to 12-month lock-in |

## Battle Card 2: LocalPulse AI vs. DIY Builders (Wix / Squarespace / WordPress)

### Competitor Overview
Self-service website builders where the business owner is expected to build, write, and maintain the site themselves.

### The Attack Vector (Where they fail)
- **The "Owner Time Trap":** A busy mechanic, salon owner, or baker doesn't have 40 hours to learn web design, mobile responsiveness, and DNS settings.
- **Looks Amateur:** DIY templates look generic, lack persuasive copywriting, and fail to rank on local Google search.
- **No Inbound/Outbound Automation:** No AI video avatars, no phone answering agents, no outbound lead scraper.

### Landmine Questions
- *"How many hours have you spent fiddling with Wix templates when you could have been running your business?"*
- *"Does your Wix site pick up the phone when a customer calls on a Saturday night?"*

## Battle Card 3: LocalPulse AI vs. Pay-Per-Lead Aggregators (Yelp, Thumbtack, Angi)

### Competitor Overview
Shared lead platforms charging $30–$90 per lead, where the same lead is sent to 5 competing shops simultaneously.

### The Attack Vector (Where they fail)
- **Rented Ground:** You build their platform, not your brand. If you stop paying Yelp $800/mo, your traffic drops to zero.
- **Price War Bidding:** Leads are shared with 4 other local competitors, triggering a race to the bottom on price.
- **Poor Lead Quality:** High spam rate and tire-kickers.

### The Reframe
> *"With Yelp or Angi, you are renting leads and competing in a price war. With LocalPulse AI, you own an automated digital asset that generates exclusive 1-on-1 customers directly for your shop at a flat, predictable cost."*

## Battle Card 4: LocalPulse AI vs. Status Quo ("We just use our Facebook Page")

### The Attack Vector
- **Invisible to High-Intent Buyers:** People search on Google Maps for *"emergency brake repair"* or *"donut catering near me"*, not Facebook.
- **Zero Automated Booking or Answering:** Facebook messages go unread for hours.

### The Reframe
> *"Facebook is great for social updates, but when someone breaks down on the highway or needs a corporate catering box for tomorrow morning, they search on Google. Without an AI website that answers calls and quotes prices instantly, you are handing those high-paying customers to the shop down the road."*
